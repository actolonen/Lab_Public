# Microbial Growth Analysis

## Growth media

We cultivate microbial cells using various growth media that are prepared using these [protocols](./Media_Recipes/).

## Measuring microbial growth at different scales: from microliters to liters.

We measure microbial growth at different volumetric scales:

-   [Microplates](./Microplates/) to measure cultures of a few hundred microliters.
-   [Fermenters](./Fermenters/) to measure cultures of 2-5 liters.

Typically, we measure microbial growth as optical density at 600 nm (OD600). However, we can also enumerate cells by flow cytometry

-   [Flow cytometry](./Cytometry/model_cytoflex.html) method to measure bacterial cell density.
