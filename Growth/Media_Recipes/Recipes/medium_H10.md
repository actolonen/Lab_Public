# H10 medium (used for *Ruminoclostridium cellulolyticum*)

## Prepare stock solutions

-   0.5% (5 g/L) iron sulfate (Sigma F6833) solution (40X)

    -   Dilute 1 g iron sulfate in 200 ml of water

    -   Autoclave

    -   Store at 4C

-   20 % (200 g/L) cellobiose solution (100x)

    -   Dilute 6 g cellobiose in 30 ml water
    -   Filter sterilize (0.2 uM)
    -   Store at 4C

-   0.1% resazurin (Sigma R7017) solution (1000x)

    -   Dilute 10 mg of resazurin in 10 ml water
    -   Filter sterilize (0.2 uM)
    -   Store at 4C

-   Mineral solution (100x)

    -   Prepare 100 ml mineral solution (table below)

    -   Autoclave mineral solution

    -   Store at 4C

|          Compound           |   Formula   | Final concentration |
|:---------------------------:|:-----------:|:-------------------:|
|      Ammonium sulfate       | (NH4)2\*SO4 |       130 g/L       |
|     Magnesium chloride      | MgCl2\*6H2O |       10 g/L        |
|      Calcium chloride       | CaCl2\*2H2O |        2 g/L        |
| Iron sulfate solution (40x) | FeSO4\*7H2O |       25 ml/L       |

## Prepare base medium

|          Compound          | Final concentration |
|:--------------------------:|:-------------------:|
|            MOPS            |   78 mM (18 g/L)    |
|           KH2PO4           |   8 mM (2.17 g/L)   |
|       Yeast extract        |        2 g/L        |
|       L-cysteine HCl       |       0.5 g/L       |
| Resazurin solution (1000x) |   1 ml per liter    |
|  Mineral solution (100x)   |   10 ml per liter   |

-   Autoclave base medium

-   Add 10 ml of cellobiose solution per liter of base medium
